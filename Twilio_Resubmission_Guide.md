# Twilio Brand & Campaign Resubmission Guide

Use the following information to fill out your Twilio A2P 10DLC Campaign resubmission.

## 1. Campaign Information

**Campaign Use Case:** Low Volume Standard (or Mixed / Marketing depending on your selection)
**Description:**
> This campaign sends account notifications, security verification codes (2FA), and requested service updates to CloudBaud clients. Messages include project status alerts, appointment reminders, and customer support responses initiated by the user.

## 2. Message Flow (Sample Messages)

**Sample Message 1 (2FA/Verification):**
> CloudBaud: Your security code is 123456. This code expires in 10 minutes. Don't share it. Reply STOP to opt out.

**Sample Message 2 (Service Update):**
> CloudBaud: Your project "Data Migration" has been updated. Status: Completed. Log in to view details: <https://cloudbaud.com/login>. Reply STOP to unsubscribe.

**Sample Message 3 (Customer Support):**
> CloudBaud: Thanks for contacting support. A specialist will review your ticket #9921 and respond shortly. Reply HELP for help, STOP to cancel.

## 3. Opt-In Details (CRITICAL)

**Opt-In Keywords:** START, SUBSCRIBE

**Opt-In Message:**
> CloudBaud: You have opted in to receive account updates and service notifications. Message frequency varies. Message & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel or HELP for help.

**Opt-In Workflow Description:**
> Users opt in via our website signup form at <https://cloudbaud.com/signup>. Users must enter their phone number and purely satisfy a mandatory checkbox that states: "I agree to the Terms and Privacy Policy. By checking this box, I consent to receive SMS/MMS texts from CloudBaud regarding my account and services..." This checkbox is not pre-selected and is required to submit the form. Users can also opt-in by texting START to our number.

## 4. Subscriber Help and Opt-Out

**Opt-Out Message:**
> CloudBaud: You have been unsubscribed and will no longer receive messages. Reply START to rejoin.

**Help Message:**
> CloudBaud: For assistance, visit <https://cloudbaud.com/contact> or email <support@cloudbaud.com>. Reply STOP to cancel.
